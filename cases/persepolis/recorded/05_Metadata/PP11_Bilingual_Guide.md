# 主要活动区域 · 区域全景

区域全景将宫殿高台放回更广的聚落景观：台下大型建筑向南北延展，西侧居住与工匠活动区分布在园地之间，菲鲁齐早期建筑区位于更远的西侧。北侧王陵与河流、农田提供区域背景，高台内外承担着不同活动。

区域位置和开敞形态有研究依据，住宅数量、上部建筑、田园分块及具体人口均未被本作确认。菲鲁齐较早的建筑是否在430BCE全部使用也未知。这是主要区域的解释性全景，不能视为所有活动已知、每座房屋均已测绘的完整城市图。

The regional panorama places the royal terrace within a wider settlement landscape: large buildings extend below it, western living and craft clusters alternate with gardens, and older Firuzi monuments lie farther west. Northern cliffs and cultivated land provide the broader setting.

Sector locations and open landscape organisation have research support. Individual houses, density, complete upper structures and430BCE occupancy remain hypothetical. This is a panorama of principal regions, not an exhaustive contemporary city survey.
