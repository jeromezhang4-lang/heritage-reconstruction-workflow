# PP09 王宫台基与私庭

南部王宫从公共柱厅转向多层台基、较小门廊和内院。塔查拉、哈迪什与中央连接空间形成一组可相互辨认的体量，远处仍能看到大型柱厅的背景。

已知位置和柱网与失去的上部建筑分开处理。PalaceH的早期阶段存在争议，本作只采用假设体块，不复制后来王朝加建的雕刻阶梯。侍从执杯或携物只是使用情境，不能证明一次具体宴饮。

## Royal palaces and raised courts

Southern royal palaces use separate podiums, smaller porticoes and courts, while the public halls remain in the background. Tachara, Hadish and the central connection are distinct masses.

Lost upper structures and disputed early Palace H phases remain explicit hypotheses. Attendants carrying vessels illustrate use rather than establish a particular feast.

证据项目：T10, T11, T12, T14, T15, T19。
