# PP04 万国门的巨型门廊

万国门的近景强调厚重门框与高大有顶空间。石构守护兽应属于门洞自身的雕刻构件，门内柱列支承木梁，人站在下方才显出其体量。

四柱厅、矩形门洞和保存柱件提供约束；完整泥砖墙、梁架和配色仍需推定。不能把图中的装饰纹样作为铭文摹本，也不以画面确认全部构件的精确尺寸。

## The monumental Gate of All Lands

The gate view stresses massive rectangular portals and the height of the roofed hall. Guardian animals belong to carved doorway elements rather than an invented line of independent statues.

The four-column plan constrains the scene; complete walls, roof framing and colour remain reconstructed. Decorative marks are not authenticated inscription transcriptions.

证据项目：T04, T05, T16, T17。
